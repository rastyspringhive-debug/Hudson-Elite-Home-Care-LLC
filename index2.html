FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?
We provide premier home care services throughout Arlington, NY and the surrounding Hudson Valley region. Contact us to see if we have caregivers available in your specific neighborhood.
How do you select your caregivers?
Our caregivers undergo a rigorous screening process. This includes comprehensive national background checks, detailed reference checks, and multiple interviews to ensure they meet our high standards for compassionate, reliable, and professional care.
Can we choose our caregiver?
Yes! We believe the caregiver-client relationship is crucial. We carefully match caregivers based on your loved one's specific needs, schedule, and personality. If you ever feel it's not a perfect fit, we are always happy to arrange a new match.
Do you require a long-term contract?
No, we offer flexible care plans without any restrictive long-term commitments. Whether you need a few hours a week for respite care or 24/7 round-the-clock support, we can customize a schedule that works for your family's unique situation.
