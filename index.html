WELCOME TO HUDSON ELITE HOME CARE LLC

COMPASSIONATE HOME CARE, CENTERED AROUND YOU

At Hudson Elite Home Care LLC, we believe everyone deserves to feel comfortable, respected, and supported in the place they call home.

We provide compassionate and dependable non-clinical home-care support designed around the individual needs and preferences of our clients.

Whether you or a loved one needs companionship, personal-care assistance, homemaking support, or respite care, our goal is to make everyday life more comfortable while supporting independence and dignity.

Serving individuals and families in Poughkeepsie and surrounding communities in Dutchess County, New York.
